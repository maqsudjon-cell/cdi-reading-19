What is in Reading Test 19

Three Academic reading passages, forty questions, sixty minutes — the same shape, order and timing as the computer-delivered IELTS Reading paper.

  1. Cosmetics in Ancient Past — Questions 1–13
  2. Children's comprehension of television advertising — Questions 14–26
  3. The geography of taste buds — Questions 27–40

The timer, the split passage-and-question layout and the highlighter behave as they do on exam day. Answers are marked the moment you submit, and the review opens every question next to the correct answer, so you can see which passage actually cost you the marks and what your raw score converts to in bands.

Free, no sign-up, and you can retake it as often as you like.

Reading Test Instructions

Read carefully before you begin. The 60-minute timer starts when you click Begin Test.

  • Read 3 passages and answer 40 questions in 60 minutes.
  • The passage is on the left; questions on the right. Drag the centre divider to resize.
  • Select text, then click the floating Highlight button. Click a highlight to remove it.
  • Use the Next → arrow or a question number in the footer to move. Switch passages with the passage cells.
  • Spell-check is disabled on inputs, as in the real exam.
  • The timer turns orange at 10 min and flashes red at 5 min. Auto-submits at 0:00.
  • After the last question, a Review screen lets you return before final submission.
